Diets

SIMPLE FOOD PLANS

Beginner diet ideas for bulking, cutting, maintaining weight, and eating cleaner.

DIET-001

Budget Bulk

Goal: gain weight and muscle. Focus on rice, eggs, chicken, oats, peanut butter, tuna, pasta, beans, milk, and Greek yogurt.

DIET-002

Simple Cut

Goal: lose fat. Focus on lean protein, vegetables, potatoes, fruit, water, and controlling snacks/drinks.

DIET-003

Maintain Weight

Goal: keep weight stable. Eat balanced meals with protein, carbs, fats, and consistent portions.

DIET-004

Clean Beginner Meals

Examples: chicken rice bowl, tuna wrap, egg sandwich, protein oatmeal, turkey burger, and Greek yogurt bowl.
