Supplements

BASIC SUPPLEMENT GUIDE

Simple supplement info based on common fitness goals. This page is education only, not medical advice.

SUP-001

Protein Powder

Useful when someone struggles to hit enough protein from food.

SUP-002

Creatine

Commonly used for strength, power, and muscle performance.

SUP-003

Electrolytes

Can help hydration, especially when sweating a lot or training hard.

SUP-004

Caffeine

Used for energy and focus. Best used carefully because too much can cause jitters.
